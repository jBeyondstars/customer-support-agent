# Nomad Cycles warranty terms

Version of March 2026. These terms apply to every product bought from Nomad Cycles, online or at the workshop.

## 1. Legal guarantees

All products are covered by the legal guarantee of conformity for 2 years from delivery (articles L217-3 and following of the French Consumer Code), and by the guarantee against hidden defects (articles 1641 and following of the Civil Code). The commercial warranties below come on top of these, they never replace them.

## 2. Nomad frames and forks

Nomad frames are covered for 5 years from delivery against manufacturing defects. This applies to the original owner only.

Rigid forks are covered for 5 years like the frame. Suspension forks follow the manufacturer's warranty, see section 4.

## 3. E-bike motor, battery and electronics

Motor, display and controller: 2 years.

Battery: 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. During that period the battery is considered defective if its capacity drops below 60% of the nominal capacity. Losing some capacity over time is normal wear and is not a defect.

## 4. Other components

Gears, brakes, wheels, suspension forks and the other components follow their manufacturer's warranty, which is 2 years in most cases. You don't have to deal with the manufacturer yourself, we handle the claim.

## 5. What is not covered

- Wear parts: tyres, inner tubes, brake pads and discs, chain, cassette, chainrings, grips, bar tape and saddle cover.
- Damage caused by a crash, a fall, an accident or bad transport (bike racks included).
- Damage caused by a lack of maintenance. Skipping the first service does not void the warranty, but problems caused by it are not covered.
- Any modification not approved by us, in particular any speed tuning device on an e-bike. Tuning voids the motor and battery warranty.
- Commercial use such as rental fleets or delivery work, except for the Cargo E9, which is covered for professional use.
- Cosmetic defects (paint, scratches) reported more than 30 days after delivery.

## 6. How to make a claim

Contact customer service with your order number, a short description of the problem and photos. For noises, a short video helps a lot.

We may ask you to bring the bike to the workshop or to a partner shop for an inspection. If the problem is covered, the repair or replacement is free, transport included. If the inspection shows the problem is not covered, the inspection is charged EUR 39.

We give you an answer within 10 working days after the inspection.

## 7. Selling your bike

The 5-year frame warranty is not transferable. The legal guarantee of conformity stays with the product for its remaining duration.

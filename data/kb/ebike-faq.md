# E-bike FAQ

## How fast do they go?

The motor helps up to 25 km/h, which is the legal limit for e-bikes in the EU. You can go faster, it's just your legs after that.

## What range will I get?

It depends a lot on how you ride. On the Urban E5 we've seen 50 km in winter on the highest assist level with hills and a heavy rider, and up to 110 km in eco mode on flat roads in summer. Cold alone can take 20 to 30% off.

What matters most: assist level, total weight (you plus luggage), hills, tyre pressure, temperature, and how often you stop and restart.

## How long does charging take?

About 5 hours from empty with the standard 4A charger, on all three models. The Cargo E9 with a second battery needs 5 hours per battery.

Charge at room temperature. If you've just ridden in freezing weather, let the battery warm up for an hour first.

The battery comes out with the bike key, so you can charge it indoors. The Trail E7 battery is integrated in the frame but it also comes out, from the bottom of the down tube.

## Can I ride in the rain?

Yes. Motor, battery and display are IP65, rain is fine. Don't use a pressure washer and don't ride through water deep enough to reach the motor.

## Do I need a licence or insurance?

No licence and no registration for a 25 km/h e-bike in France. Insurance isn't mandatory either, but check your home insurance: most of them don't cover theft outside your home.

## Can you unlock the speed limit?

No. And please don't fit a tuning kit. It's illegal on public roads (legally the bike becomes a moped) and it voids the motor and battery warranty.

## Can I get a purchase grant?

Some cities and regions help with buying an e-bike. Rules change often, so check with your local authority. We can provide the invoice and the certificate of conformity they usually ask for.

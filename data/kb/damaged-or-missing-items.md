# Damaged parcels and missing items

## The box looks damaged on arrival

Check it in front of the driver. If it's badly crushed or open, you can refuse it: it comes back to us and we send a new one.

If you accept it, write precise reservations on the delivery slip, like "box torn on the left side, wheel visible". A plain "subject to checking" doesn't count for the carriers.

## Something inside is damaged

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the item, the box and the shipping label. After 48 hours carriers turn down almost every claim, so we can't promise anything either.

For bikes, don't ride it if anything structural looks wrong (frame, fork, wheels, brakes). We'll arrange a pickup, or you can bring it to the workshop if you live near Lyon.

Damaged items are replaced or refunded in full, shipping included. You don't pay anything for the return.

## Something is missing

Bikes sometimes ship with small parts (pedals, reflectors, the e-bike charger) in a second box taped to the main one. Check all the packaging first. If it's really missing, tell us within 7 days and we'll send it.

For accessories, look at the shipping email: a large order can be split into two parcels, each with its own tracking number.

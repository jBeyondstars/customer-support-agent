# Returns and refunds

You have 30 days from the delivery date to ask for a return. The law says 14, we give you 30.

## What can be returned

Most things, as long as they're unused and in their original packaging with the labels on.

The exceptions:

- Nutrition (gels, bars, drink mixes). Food safety, no way around it.
- Gift cards.
- Custom builds. They're built to your spec, so they can only come back if they're faulty.
- Spare e-bike batteries, unless the seal on the box is still intact.

Bikes and e-bikes can be returned if they've done less than 20 km. We check the odometer on e-bikes. Take the pedals off and pack the bike in its original box. Honestly, keep the box for 30 days, it makes everything easier.

Helmets can be returned if they've never been worn, with the stickers on and the foam pads still in their bag.

## How to return something

1. Ask for a return from your account, or through the chat assistant on the site. Tell us which items and why.
2. We email you a prepaid label within one working day.
3. Drop the parcel at a post office or a Colissimo pickup point.

For bikes we book a pickup with GEODIS. It costs €49, taken off your refund. If the bike is faulty or we sent the wrong one, the pickup is free.

Returning accessories from mainland France is free. From elsewhere in the EU, the return shipping is on you.

## Refunds

We refund within 14 days after the return reaches us, on the original payment method. If you send back the whole order, the original shipping cost is refunded too.

Paid with Alma? The remaining instalments are cancelled and what you've already paid comes back to your card.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. If you need another size or colour, return the first item and place a new order. We can put the new one aside while the return is on its way, just ask.

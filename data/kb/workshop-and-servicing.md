# Workshop and servicing

Our workshop is at 12 rue des Ateliers, 69100 Villeurbanne. Bikes are built there, and we also service and repair them.

## Free first service

Every bike bought from us gets a free first service, either after 300 km or within 3 months, whichever comes first. Cables stretch and bolts settle on a new bike, so it's worth doing.

Not near Lyon? We have partner shops in most big cities. The list comes with your order, or just ask us.

## Repairs and servicing

Book a slot online or by phone. We work on any brand, except e-bike motors from brands we don't sell.

- Standard service: €69
- Full service (everything stripped, cleaned and adjusted): €129
- E-bike diagnostic: €39, taken off the bill if you go ahead with the repair

It usually takes 2 to 4 working days, a bit longer in spring when everyone remembers their bike at the same time.

## Warranty repairs

Warranty repairs are free. See the warranty terms for what's covered and how to make a claim.

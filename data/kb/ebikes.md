# Our e-bikes

All three share the same display, the same app and the same 4A charger. Sizes are in the size guide.

## Nomad Urban E5 (€1,890)

Our commuter. Rear hub motor, 250 W and 45 Nm, quiet and low maintenance. Removable 504 Wh battery, 50 to 110 km of range.

- Shimano 8-speed, hydraulic disc brakes
- Integrated front and rear lights, mudguards, kickstand
- Rear rack, 25 kg max
- 23.4 kg with the battery
- Colours: sand, slate grey
- Sizes S, M, L

## Nomad Trail E7 (€3,290)

Hardtail e-MTB for trails and rough gravel. Mid-drive motor, 250 W and 85 Nm, so it climbs properly. 630 Wh battery integrated in the down tube, 40 to 90 km of range.

- 120 mm suspension fork
- 12-speed, 4-piston hydraulic brakes
- 29" wheels (27.5" on size S)
- 24.9 kg
- No rack or mudguard mounts. It's not a commuter, sorry.

## Nomad Cargo E9 (€4,490)

Longtail cargo bike for kids, shopping, or both. Mid-drive motor, 250 W and 85 Nm. Comes with one 504 Wh battery (45 to 100 km) and has a slot for a second one, sold separately.

- Carries two kids on the back with the optional seats
- 200 kg max total load, rider included
- Enviolo stepless hub, hydraulic disc brakes
- 34 kg
- One size, fits riders from 155 to 195 cm

The spare 504 Wh battery fits the Urban E5 and the Cargo E9, not the Trail E7.

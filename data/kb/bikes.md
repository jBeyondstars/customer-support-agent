# Bikes without a motor

## Nomad Gravel G3 (€1,290)

Aluminium frame, carbon fork, Shimano GRX 2x10, hydraulic disc brakes. Ships with 700x40 tyres and takes up to 45 mm. Mounts for racks, mudguards and three bottles. 9.8 kg in size M.

### Gravel G3 custom build (from €2,150)

Same frame, but you pick the groupset, the wheels and the colour. Built to order in 3 to 4 weeks. Custom builds can't be returned since they're made for you, but the warranty is the same as on any other bike.

## Nomad City C2 (€690)

Steel frame, 7-speed hub gears, so there's almost nothing to maintain. Rim brakes, mudguards, rear rack and dynamo lights. 15.2 kg. Sizes XS to L.

## Nomad Kids K1 (€390)

24" wheels, 8-speed, mechanical disc brakes, 11.1 kg. Light enough for a kid to actually enjoy riding it. For kids from about 125 to 145 cm.

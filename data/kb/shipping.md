# Shipping

Everything ships from our workshop in Villeurbanne, next to Lyon. Prices and delays below are for mainland France unless it says otherwise.

## How long will it take?

Accessories, parts and clothing ordered before 2pm on a working day leave the same day. After 2pm they go out the next working day. Colissimo usually needs 2 to 3 working days after that, DPD about the same.

Bikes are slower. Every bike is built and checked by one of our mechanics before it leaves, so count 3 to 5 working days before dispatch. GEODIS then delivers in 3 to 6 working days and calls you to book a delivery slot. If you miss the call they send a text with a link to pick a slot yourself.

Custom builds take 3 to 4 weeks before dispatch.

If your order has a bike and accessories in it, everything travels together with the bike.

Heads up for August: the workshop is closed for the first two weeks, so bikes ordered then are built when we're back. Accessories keep shipping as usual.

## Tracking

You get an email with the tracking number when the parcel is handed to the carrier. The carrier's website can take up to 24 hours to show anything, that's normal.

## My parcel hasn't moved

If the tracking hasn't changed for 5 working days, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the carrier. It takes up to 10 working days. If the parcel is declared lost, we send a new one for free or refund you, your call.

Please don't place a second order while an investigation is open. We can't stop the first parcel once it's moving and you'd end up with two.

## Workshop pickup

You can collect your order at the workshop for free, pick "Workshop pickup" at checkout. We email you when it's ready: usually the same day for accessories, 2 to 3 days for bikes. We keep it for 15 days.

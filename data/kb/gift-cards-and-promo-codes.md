# Gift cards and promo codes

## Gift cards

Gift cards come in €50 and €100 and are sent by email on the date you choose. They're valid for 12 months and can be spread over several orders, online or at the workshop.

They can't be refunded, swapped for cash or returned. If one gets lost, we can resend it to the email address used at purchase.

## Promo codes

One code per order, entered at checkout. We can't add a code after the order has been placed.

Codes don't work on custom builds or on gift cards.

Signing up for the newsletter gets you 10% off your first accessories order.

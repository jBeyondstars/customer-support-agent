# Your account and your data

## Account

You can change your email address, password and delivery addresses in your account. Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" on the login page, the link is valid for one hour.

You don't need an account to order, but you need one to follow your orders and ask for returns online.

## Deleting your account

Email us and we'll delete it within 30 days. We have to keep invoices for 10 years (that's French accounting law), but everything else goes.

## What we do with your data

We use it to deliver your orders, handle returns and warranty claims, and answer your questions. We don't sell it, ever.

Newsletter: only if you said yes, and there's an unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email.

## The chat assistant

The assistant only sees your own orders, deliveries and returns, and only while you're logged in. It never sees your payment details. Conversations are kept for 6 months so the team can follow up if it hands you over to them.

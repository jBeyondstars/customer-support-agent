# Size guide

Most of our frames come in S, M and L, and the City C2 also comes in XS. Start from your height, then check your inseam if you're between two sizes.

| Size | Rider height | Inseam |
|---|---|---|
| XS (City C2 only) | 150 to 160 cm | 68 to 74 cm |
| S | 158 to 170 cm | 72 to 79 cm |
| M | 168 to 181 cm | 78 to 85 cm |
| L | 179 to 195 cm | 84 to 92 cm |

Between two sizes? Go smaller if you want a more upright and comfortable position, bigger if you ride long distances and like to stretch out.

The Cargo E9 is one size and adjusts for riders from 155 to 195 cm.

The Kids K1 has 24" wheels and fits kids from about 125 to 145 cm, so roughly 8 to 11 years old.

Still not sure? Come and try them at the workshop, or send us your height and inseam and we'll tell you what we'd pick.

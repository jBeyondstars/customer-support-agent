# Orders and payment

## Changing or cancelling an order

While your order is "processing" we can still change the delivery address or cancel it. Contact us and we'll do it, there's no button for it yet.

Once it has shipped it's too late to cancel. You'll have to return it when it arrives (see Returns and refunds).

Bikes stay in "processing" for a few days while the workshop builds them, so you usually have a bit of time.

## Order statuses

- Processing: we've got your order and we're preparing it (or building it, for bikes).
- Shipped: it's with the carrier and you have a tracking number.
- Delivered: the carrier says it's been delivered.
- Cancelled: cancelled by you or by us (failed payment, out of stock). If you were charged, the refund is automatic within 5 working days.

## Payment methods

Visa, Mastercard, CB, Amex, PayPal and Apple Pay.

Bank transfer works for orders over €1,000. We hold the order until the money arrives (1 to 3 working days) and cancel it after 10 days without payment.

You can pay in 3 or 4 instalments with Alma for orders between €300 and €4,000. 3x is free, 4x has a small fee shown at checkout.

Your card is charged when you place the order, not when it ships.

## Invoices

The invoice is emailed when the order ships and it's also in your account. Need a company name or a VAT number on it? Tell us before it ships, we can't edit invoices afterwards.
